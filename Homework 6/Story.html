My Favorite Story

Little Red Riding Hood

(summary)

The Mission and the Warning

The story follows a young girl who always wears a red hooded cloak. One day, her mother asks her to deliver a basket of treats to her grandmother, who lives deep in the forest and is feeling unwell. Her mother gives her a strict warning: "Stay on the path and do not talk to strangers."


The Encounter

While walking through the woods, she meets a Big Bad Wolf. Because she is young and innocent, she forgets her mother's warning and tells the wolf exactly where she is going. The clever wolf suggests she pick some flowers for her grandmother, giving him enough time to race ahead to the cottage.


The Deception

The wolf reaches the house first,locks her in a closet, and puts on her nightgown and cap. When Little Red Riding Hood arrives, she is confused by her grandmother's appearance, leading to the famous exchange:
-Little Red: "Grandmother, what big eyes you have!"
-Wolf: "All the better to see you with, my dear."
-Little Red: "Grandmother, what big ears you have!"
-Wolf: "All the better to hear you with."
-Little Red: "Grandmother, what big teeth you have!"
-Wolf: "All the better to eat you with!"


The Rescue

Just as the wolf leaps to attack, a brave woodcutter (or hunter) who was nearby hears the girl's screams. He rushes into the cottage, defeats the wolf, and saves Little Red Riding Hood and her grandmother. The girl learns a valuable lesson about the importance of listening to her parents and being cautious of strangers.


Wisdoms Learned

  • Safety First: Never share personal details with people you don't know.
  • Obedience: Rules are often there for our protection.
  • Resilience: Help can come from unexpected places when we face danger.
